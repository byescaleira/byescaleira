## `// about`

I am **Rafael Escaleira**, an iOS Specialist building the **Cartola FC** app at **Globo** — the largest fantasy football game in Brazil. I operate at the intersection of native Apple craft, clean architecture, and product thinking.

My work spans SwiftUI/UIKit interfaces, modular codebases with SPM, CI/CD pipelines, design systems, and performance optimization for millions of users. I use AI as an engineering accelerator, never as a replacement for judgment.

- **Role:** iOS Specialist
- **Company:** Globo — Cartola FC
- **Location:** Campo Grande, MS / Rio de Janeiro, Brazil
- **Experience:** 7+ years
- **Education:**
  - BTech Computer Systems Analysis — Descomplica (2023–2025)
  - BSc Computer Engineering — UFMS (2017–2021)
